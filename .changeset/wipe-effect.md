Add a `wipe` transition variant that reveals the new theme by animating `clip-path` across the viewport, configurable via the `direction` option alongside the existing `duration` and `easing` overrides.

`direction` accepts:

- **Edges**: `left`, `right`, `up`, `down`
- **Center**: `center-x`, `center-y`
- **Corners**: `diagonal-tl`, `diagonal-tr`, `diagonal-bl`, `diagonal-br`
